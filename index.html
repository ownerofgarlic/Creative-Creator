Why Shalva?

Shalva is a leading nonprofit dedicated to the inclusion and care of people with disabilities, from infancy through adulthood — regardless of religion, gender, or ethnic background.

Inclusive Care

Serves thousands of children, teens, and adults, welcoming every family without exception.

Comprehensive Support

Delivers therapies, inclusive education, vocational training, and family support programs.

Proven Track Record

Over three decades of continuous care and community advocacy in Israel and beyond.
